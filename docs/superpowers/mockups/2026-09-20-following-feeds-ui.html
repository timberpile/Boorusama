Browse, then manage

Open a feed to see its saved recent posts immediately. Older results load as you approach the end. The freshness line reflects gradual source updates.

  • Following count shows how many feeds contain the exact artist tag or query.
  • Manage shows each tracked search with its own status, open action and refresh action.
  • NEW belongs to a feed when one of its tracked searches has NEW posts.
