Centered media, attached details, separate image and Work navigation. Try your own images locally. Names, gestures and zoom constants are candidates, not approved defaults.

Your Images

Each selection creates one Work. Select again to create another Work and test skipping. Up to 30 files per selection, 60 in total, 25 MB per file.

No images leave this browser.

PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, AVIF and BMP when supported by your browser. No uploads, storage or network requests. Reloading clears local images.

Preview Setup

Tap the image: Image View / Overview.
Double-tap: zoom / reset.
Swipe image: previous / next image.
Swipe the title/description in Overview: previous / next Work. The page counter remains visible for multi-page Works.

Desktop Test Controls

Two Navigation Levels

Work A: Image 1 ↔ Image 2 ↔ Image 3
↕ deliberate Work change
Work B: Image 1 ↔ Image 2

One ordinary image swipe cannot unambiguously mean both “next image” and “skip this whole Work”.

Baseline experiment: tap to Overview, then swipe its title/description to skip. Two-finger gestures are reserved for pinch zoom, never Work skipping. Real Android pinch, TalkBack and system gestures still require testing. The snap tolerance experiment defaults to 10%, double the current 5% Flutter value.
