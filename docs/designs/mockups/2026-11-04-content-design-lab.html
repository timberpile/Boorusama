What This Tests

A square image fits height in a portrait viewport. Nearby portrait ratios get a smaller step; wide images get more. Very wide images reveal the tradeoff between exact fit and a cap.

q = max(imageRatio / viewportRatio, viewportRatio / imageRatio). Production must additionally preserve existing pinch limits, media-quality changes, video seek, profile gesture overrides and actual usable viewport dimensions.
