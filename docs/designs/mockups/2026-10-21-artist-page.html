B · Search Shortcut

Based on your feedback

Artist information stays compact. Search opens the regular search in a separate app view with asano_mio prefilled.

Artist → Normal Search → BackArtist sorting and scroll position stay intact. Searching uses the same profile, but its query is independent.
Kept on Artist Page
  • Follow, not “Follow Topic”.
  • Newest / Popular beside the Posts heading.
  • A labeled Search shortcut, not a settings/filter icon.
  • No extra search field, filter sheet, or Browse Posts action.
  • Links, aliases, and wiki access remain above the posts.
Try it: select Popular, scroll, open Search, add scenery or -comic, then use Back. The artist view stays as you left it. Switch between mobile/desktop and light/dark.

Note: this is an interaction mockup, not production UI. Search and wiki content are illustrative; no live requests are made.
