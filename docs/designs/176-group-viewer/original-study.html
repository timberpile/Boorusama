Design rationale

Visible choices, stable context.

A labelled pair makes the two presentations explicit, without turning Timeline into an on/off setting.

01 / SAME MEMBERS

Mode is not order.

Single and Timeline use the same member sequence. Related groups expose sorting and a local custom order in both modes.

02 / CLEAR BOUNDARIES

Pages stay inside the group.

Member arrows stop at the ends. Explicit feed navigation is separate. This is a proposed change to current edge navigation.

03 / SOURCE MEANING

Keep authored sequences intact.

Series use Ordered. Pixiv uses author page order. Missing members keep their place. Pools retain their sort choices.

Study controls, not app settings. This frame is a code-grounded approximation, not an Android screenshot. Media taps hide chrome; ← → move members; Esc dismisses a sheet or returns.
