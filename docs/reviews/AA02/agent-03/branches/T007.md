## Findings and uncertainty

No independently confirmed actionable defect. **Compatibility risk (not a proven defect):** `_canLoadMore` will stop if `result.total` is non-null and no larger than the accumulated distinct count even when the actual service's total is only a page-local/approximate value. The core contract does not mark `total` as authoritative. A targeted engine audit is needed to confirm whether any active implementation returns such a value without `hasMore`/`maxPage`. Do not count this as a confirmed bug absent a reachable producer.
