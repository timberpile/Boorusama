## Findings

**No standalone source-level defect established in the inspected scope.** Synchronous sheet collapse occurs on actual page-change notification, not on every gesture or failed swipe. The resulting PageView physics remains disabled while zoomed, global swipe-disabled, multi-pointer interacting, or details animation in progress, and remains blocked for vertical/large-screen expanded presentation.

Because issue #104 is an explicit gesture **prototype**, a source-only conclusion does not establish that nested controls, text selection and horizontal metadata scrollers actually win their gesture arenas. This behavioral acceptance is a deliberate manual-device gate rather than proof of a bug from the diff alone.
