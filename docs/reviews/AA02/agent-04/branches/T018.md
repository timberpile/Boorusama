## Requirements and implementation

The Post Grid's count and actions stay on one compact toolbar; distinct posts hidden by active blacklist rules are counted once. Tapping even a zero-hidden indicator opens a bounded anchored popup with per-rule toggles, Show All/Hide All; horizontal contents scroll while the rightmost overflow stays fixed. A scrolling `SliverFloatingHeader` hides on downward scroll and reappears on upward scroll, with a separately persisted Keep Timeline Toolbar Visible switch (off by default); preexisting show-header setting is never treated as this new pinning value.
